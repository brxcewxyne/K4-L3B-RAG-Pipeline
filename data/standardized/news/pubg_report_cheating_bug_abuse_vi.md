## I found a player abusing a bug. How do I report it?

*Updated August 03, 2020. Source: https://support.pubg.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004197034-I-found-a-player-abusing-a-bug-How-do-I-report-it*

We are focused on removing all users who are creating an unfair gaming environment by improving our anti-cheat system. Our In-game reporting system is the best option to report these users, as it will be forwarded instantly to our team. You will be notified in-game if there is any action taken against the suspected player. Please go take a look at the link below to know how the new reporting system works:

https://youtu.be/mbuHBkcEnNw

You can also reach out to our support team with the following details:

- Type of Violation (Aimbot, ESP, Wallhack, etc.)
- Date and Time of the incident
- Name of the suspected player(s)
- A video of the issue as an email attachment (not exceeding 20 MB) or a video link describing the same

If you have reported the player via the in-game reporting system, you will receive a notification about the result of the investigation within 15 days.
