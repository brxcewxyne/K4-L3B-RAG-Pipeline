# If someone else uses cheats/hacks on my account, will I get banned? Can it be removed? — PUBG Support

## If someone else uses cheats/hacks on my account, will I get banned? Can it be removed?

*Updated August 03, 2020.*

Safeguarding your game account is your responsibility and PUBG is not liable if someone else has access to your account and have misused the account. Please do not share your Steam credentials to anyone.

Therefore, regardless of who had cheated or violated the terms and services on the account, the game account will be the one that is restricted.

Bans on accounts cannot be reversed once evidence of a violation is determined.
