# I want to report a site or user who is selling cheats/hacks — PUBG Support

## I want to report a site or user who is selling cheats/hacks

*Updated August 03, 2020.*

Your proactive vigilance in keeping the game fair is highly appreciated.

We are aware that there are vendors selling game hacks through various channels including social media and streaming platforms.

Our team is working on blocking out those users, websites, cheats etc. and improving our systems to detect these infractions.

If you come across websites containing cheats, hacks, and scams for our games, you can help us by raising an email to our support team with the following information:
